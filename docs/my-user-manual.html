How it works

One slide at a time. Everyone takes part.

  1. Open the presenter screen.A room code and QR code appear on the projector.
  2. Students scan with their phones.No sign-up. Each joins as a random orchestra instrument, the only name shown on screen.
  3. Advance one slide at a time.Phones follow along automatically.
  4. Students answer on question slides.Answers appear on the big screen without names. Turn on review mode to approve each one first.
  5. The first-page worksheet stays private.It stays on each phone and can be downloaded.
  6. Hear the orchestra.Click each instrument to hear its part alone, then all together, before the closing video.
  7. Finish with a review.Students share what they learned, review the lecture, unlock their 20-question results, and write how they’ll use their strengths and weaknesses.
