About

What’s in a name.

“You will be called Hephzibah — my delight is in her.”Isaiah 62:4 (paraphrased)

Hephzibah means “my delight is in her.” We believe every student is already precious before God — not because of grades or usefulness, but because they are known and loved.

Hephzibah Academy creates short, honest lectures and reflection activities that help young people ask good questions about their future, and take one small next step.
